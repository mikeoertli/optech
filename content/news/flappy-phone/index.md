Flappy Bird, you've met your match - [Flappy Phone](https://twitter.com/reckless/status/438031026397655040). Except they are serious, the new [Samsung Galaxy S5](http://www.theverge.com/2014/2/24/5441668/samsung-galaxy-s5-announcement-launch) has a flap over the [immensely hideous USB 3 micro B port](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Connector_USB_3_IMGP6033_wp_.jpg). On one hand, this is a "face palm" moment. On the other hand, I applaud them for avoiding the 2 hours of listing gimmick software features that no one cared about like they did at the S4 unveiling. 

Also worth mentioning is the fact that the fingerprint scanner is "quite unreliable and virtually impossible to activate," but I'm sure no one will notice (\*cough).
