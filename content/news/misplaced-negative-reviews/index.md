Apple has a tradition where they release a <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/app/id777716569" target="_blank">12 Days of Gifts app</a> then each day around Christmas they give people a free version of some paid content - apps, music, books, or videos.  You'd think people would be grateful.

Many people are grateful, presumably. However, there is an increasing number of people that are <a href="http://www.imore.com/apples-12-days-gifts-prove-giving-can-be-hard" target="_blank">leaving one star reviews</a> on apps, movies, etc. if the free content of the day isn't an app they personally wanted. Angry people leaving one star on a children's game because they don't play it sounds absurd, but sadly, is a reality.
