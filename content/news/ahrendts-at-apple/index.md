We are getting closer to Angela Ahrendts' start date at Apple (this Spring) and MacRumors has posted <a href="http://www.macrumors.com/2014/01/06/how-angela-ahrendts-burberry-experience-could-drive-the-future-of-apple-retail/" target="_blank">this nice overview</a> of how she will fit in at Apple, and more importantly what she brings to the table.
